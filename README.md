# sales-performance-analysis-sql-powerbi

# 📊 Retail Sales Performance Analysis

**Tools:** SQL, Power BI, Excel  
**Focus:** KPI reporting, trend analysis, business insights

---

## Business Context
In many retail settings, sales data is spread across regions and product categories, and without a clear, consolidated view, it can be hard to see how performance really compares. Static reports often miss early signs of underperformance or emerging trends.

This project looks at multi-region retail sales data and brings that data together in a way that helps teams understand performance patterns and make better decisions.

---

## Business Questions
I approached this analysis with a few key questions in mind:

- Which regions and product categories contribute the most to overall revenue?
- Are there regions or categories that are falling behind?
- How do key sales metrics vary across time and across regions?

---

## Data Overview
The dataset includes retail sales transactions from multiple regions. Key fields used in analysis include:

- Region  
- Product category  
- Revenue  
- Order volume  
- Time period  

I cleaned and validated the data before starting the analysis to make sure the results were reliable and consistent.

---

## Analysis Approach
I used SQL to pull together the sales data, calculate key performance measures, and identify patterns across regions and categories that weren’t obvious in the raw transaction records.

Then, I built interactive dashboards in Power BI to visualize how revenue and other KPIs change over time, compare performance between regions, and highlight trends that might need attention.

---

## Key Insights
- Some regions showed lower revenue contributions even though order volumes were steady  
- A few product categories drove most of the revenue  
- Seasonal patterns were visible across regions

---

## Outcome & Business Use Case
This work provides stakeholders with interactive dashboards that make it easier to monitor ongoing performance, compare regions, and spot areas that may need focus. These visuals support more informed discussions about sales strategies, inventory planning, and performance reporting.

---

## Deliverables
- SQL queries used for KPI and trend analysis  
- Power BI dashboards for interactive performance reporting
