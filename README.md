# sales-performance-analysis-sql-powerbi

# 📊 Retail Sales Performance Analysis

**Tools:** SQL, Power BI, Excel  
**Focus:** KPI reporting, trend analysis, business insights

---

## Business Context
In many retail environments, sales data exists across regions and product categories, but it isn’t always easy to see how performance compares or where issues are emerging. Without a clear, consolidated view, teams can miss early signs of underperformance or rely on static reports that don’t support timely decisions.

This project looks at multi-region retail sales data to create a clearer picture of overall performance and support more informed, data-driven reporting.

---

## Business Questions
This analysis was guided by a few key questions:

- Which regions and product categories contribute the most to overall revenue?
- Are there regions or categories that are consistently underperforming?
- How do sales KPIs change over time and differ across regions?

---

## Data Overview
The dataset consists of retail sales transactions across multiple regions. Key fields include:

- Region  
- Product category  
- Revenue  
- Order volume  
- Time period  

Before analysis, the data was cleaned and validated to ensure accuracy and consistency in reporting.

---

## Analysis Approach
I used SQL to aggregate sales data, calculate key performance indicators, and analyze trends at the regional and category level. This helped surface patterns that aren’t visible in raw transaction data.

Using Power BI, I then built interactive dashboards to visualize revenue trends over time, compare performance across regions and product categories, and highlight areas that may require further attention.

---

## Key Insights
- Some regions showed lower revenue contribution despite steady order volumes  
- A small number of product categories were responsible for a large share of overall revenue  
- Seasonal patterns were visible in sales performance across regions  

---

## Outcome & Business Use Case
This analysis provides stakeholders with an interactive way to monitor sales performance instead of relying on static reports. The dashboards make it easier to compare regions, spot underperforming segments, and support discussions around sales strategy, inventory planning, and reporting priorities.

---

## Deliverables
- SQL queries used for KPI and trend analysis  
- Power BI dashboards for interactive performance reporting
